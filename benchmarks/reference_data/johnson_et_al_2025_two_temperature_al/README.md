# Johnson et al. (2025) two-temperature aluminium reference data

This package contains curves associated with Fig. 2(a)--2(d) of
Z. A. Johnson, N. R. Shaffer, and M. S. Murillo, “Quantum
Ornstein-Zernike theory for two-temperature two-component plasmas,”
*Physical Review E* **112**, 025207 (2025),
[doi:10.1103/5c29-kdx1](https://doi.org/10.1103/5c29-kdx1).

All states are aluminium at `rho=2.7 g cm^-3` and `Ti=1 eV`:

| Panel | `Te` (eV) | Curves |
|---|---:|---|
| Fig. 2(a) | 1 | 2TTCP HNC+bridge, DFT-MD, YOCP HNC+bridge |
| Fig. 2(b) | 3 | 2TTCP HNC+bridge, DFT-MD, YOCP HNC+bridge |
| Fig. 2(c) | 10 | 2TTCP HNC+bridge, DFT-MD, YOCP HNC+bridge |
| Fig. 2(d) | 30 | 2TTCP HNC+bridge, DFT-MD, YOCP HNC+bridge |

Column 1 is radius in Bohr, matching the published `r [au]` axis, and column
2 is dimensionless `gii(r)`.  No smoothing or coordinate conversion has been
applied.  The project maintainer digitized the curves from Fig. 2(a)--2(d).

No open-data license is attached to these point sets.  They carry license
status `NOASSERTION` and are not covered by Otter's BSD-3-Clause software
license.  Checksums and panel assignments are recorded in `manifest.json`.
The publication PDF is not included.
